Privacy Policy — Halo

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Halo ("the app") is an astrology app that computes natal charts and offers a reflective astrologer chat. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In short: Halo is local-first — your data stays on your device, with one clearly-described exception for the optional chat feature.

Information you enter

To compute a chart you provide a name, birth date, birth time, and birth place, and optionally a gender. This information is stored only on your device (in the app's local database) so your saved charts persist between sessions. It is not uploaded to us or to any server we operate — we do not operate any server, and we have no account system.

The "Ask the Universe" chat (optional)

If you use the in-app astrologer chat, the app sends a summary of the chart you selected and the messages you type to Anthropic, PBC (the provider of the Claude AI model) in order to generate replies. This happens only when you actively use the chat. Your use of Anthropic's API is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

The chat requires your own Anthropic API key, which you enter in the app. That key is stored only on your device and is sent only to Anthropic to authenticate your requests. It is never transmitted to us or any third party.

What we do not collect

Halo contains no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no account sign-in. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. We have no access to your charts, messages, or API key.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows a daily reading generated locally on your device. No data is sent off-device for this.

Children

Halo is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your control

You can delete any saved chart in the app at any time, and you can remove your API key. Uninstalling the app removes all locally-stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
